# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers at the address in SECURITY.md. All complaints are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

The full text of the Covenant, including the enforcement guidelines, applies as published at the link above.
