### 24.8 Non-goals for this plan

To keep the plan tight:

- No new workflow engine evaluation beyond Temporal (after §24.2).
- No re-evaluation of LangGraph as the in-process runtime.
- No assistant-ui frontend work here (tracked separately as task #73).
- No Steal-1 (credential proxy), Steal-2 (smart log sampling),
  Steal-3 (semantic alert correlation) — they remain pending as their
  own tracks.
