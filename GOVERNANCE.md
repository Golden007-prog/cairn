# Governance

cairn is maintained by its core maintainers (see CONTRIBUTING.md for how to become one). Decisions that change a public contract are recorded as ADRs under `docs/decisions/`; a locked ADR is superseded by a new one, never edited. Contributions are accepted under Apache-2.0 with a DCO sign-off on every commit.

## Review turnaround

A maintainer gives every new issue and pull request a first response within five working days: a review, a question, or a label saying who picks it up. Pull requests are routed by `.github/CODEOWNERS`; a pull request merges after a maintainer's approval, the required CI checks and the DCO check.
